## Challenge
Planet X and SPHEREx — NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

## What it does
- Shows real NASA SPHEREx near-infrared images of the same patch of sky at different times (blink comparator).
- Automatically flags sources that moved, checks them against known solar-system objects (JPL, IMCCE SkyBoT)
  and explains each one with an evidence report: positions, motion, rough distance, 0.75–5 µm spectrum.
- Measures what SPHEREx can and cannot detect (injection–recovery), and puts the Planet X hypothesis in context.
